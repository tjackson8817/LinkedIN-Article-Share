LinkedIn Article Share Builder

Finds the top 3 on-brand, recent articles worth sharing — ranked automatically — and drafts two genuinely different posts for each, so you're choosing between real options, not just approving one draft.

Sixth tool in this family, but a different shape. This one isn't part of the job-search funnel — it's for staying visible on LinkedIn and building a thought-leadership position, between job-search activity or independent of it entirely.
1 · What counts as "on-brand"

This is what makes "impactful" mean something specific to you rather than generic trending news — be as specific as your actual niche.

Shapes which angle counts as impactful — a recruiter-facing post and a client-facing post can reasonably favor different picks from the same pool.

Thought leadership compounds across posts that connect to a few recognizable throughlines, not one-off reactions to whatever's trending. This also feeds the hashtags below, so they help build a consistent community of interest around you, not just tag each article in isolation.

2 · Your Brand Identity

This is what grounds the Position/Stance variant below in something real — without it, that variant stays more general rather than inventing a personal claim or credential you didn't give it. See the guardrail note further down.

These fields aren't remembered automatically — closing the tab clears them, same as every other field here. Save downloads a small file with your name/tagline/eyebrow/background/themes; Load reads one back in. Keep the file somewhere handy and reload it each time instead of retyping.

3 · Options

Artwork is generated as a separate follow-up step, only for the one post you actually pick — not for all 6 drafts upfront. Downloadable .png in the same visual style used across this tool family. Only works in Claude with Code execution and file creation enabled.

Step 1's text always comes back as a downloadable Word document (.docx) — one section per article (context, then Variant A, then Variant B), clearly labeled and visually separated so all 3 articles and 6 drafts are easy to compare side by side. Requires Code execution and file creation.

Built-in honesty guardrails, not optional: only real, verifiable articles with real URLs — no invented sources. Every summary is written fresh in Claude's own words, never copied from the source. If genuinely on-brand, recent, free-to-read articles are thin on a given day, the tool reports fewer than 3 and says so rather than padding the list.
The Position/Stance variant stays grounded too: it only draws on the background/themes you actually gave it. With nothing given, it stays more general commentary rather than inventing a personal claim, credential, or experience to sound more authoritative.
